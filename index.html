THE GAP

Technology moves faster than our understanding of its human effects.

A digital ID system, a new health platform, an AI-driven public service. Each can reach a population and change daily life long before anyone has really asked how. That gap, between a technology's arrival and its lived effect on people, is the space we work in.

We call it liminal because it is unresolved. Neither the old way of life nor the new one has fully taken hold.

WHAT WE DO

Insight, made actionable, and carried through.

01

Insight discovery

We spend real time with the people a technology is meant to reach, not surveys from a distance. We look at how a system actually changes daily routines, trust, and power.

02

Actionable recommendations

Field findings become specific guidance an institution can use before or during rollout: what to adjust, what to watch for, who is at risk of being left out.

03

Implementation

When a project needs more than a report, we bring in the right specialists, anthropologists and technologists, to help see the changes through.

HOW IT WORKS

A method, in four steps.

01

Embed

We spend time with the population a technology will affect, long enough to see real patterns, not a snapshot.

02

Understand

We surface how the technology actually changes daily life, the effects an institution didn't anticipate, positive and negative.

03

Translate

Findings become recommendations specific enough to change a rollout plan, not general observations.

04

Implement

Where needed, we bring in the right people to carry the changes through, not just hand over a report.

WHO IT'S FOR

Institutions with real human stakes in a rollout.

PRIMARY

Development banks and funders

Conducting feasibility studies, safeguards assessments, or social due diligence before financing a project that will touch people's everyday lives, a digital ID rollout, a health platform, a large infrastructure project.

Governments

Preparing to roll out a digital ID system, a health platform, or another service that will reach the population at scale.

Corporations

Assessing how a new product might affect a vulnerable population before entering a new market.
